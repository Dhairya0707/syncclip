## 2. BRAND VOICE — HYBRID STRATEGY

SyncClip has **two voices** depending on context:

| Channel | Voice | Personality |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **LinkedIn (Personal)** | **Founder — Dhairya** | Humble, authentic, "building in public." Shares real struggles and wins. Talks like a student who shipped something real. |
| **LinkedIn (Company)** | **Product — SyncClip** | Authoritative, editorial, concise. Reads like a tech journal. |

**For LinkedIn personal posts (which is what you'll write most):**

### Voice Pillars
1. **Confident, not arrogant** — State facts without hype. "Sub-100ms sync. No accounts required." NOT "The world's best clipboard."
2. **Precise, not jargon-heavy** — "Type on your phone, see it on your laptop. Instantly." NOT "Leveraging our robust WebSocket ecosystem."
3. **Transparent builder** — Share the real process, including struggles. "DNS propagation took 45 minutes and I panicked."
4. **Invite conversation** — End with a question. Ask for feedback, roasts, or experiences. Never just announce.

### Banned Words (Never Use)
seamless · robust · best-in-class · synergize · solution · empower · paradigm · leverage · cutting-edge · next-gen · scalable · enterprise-grade · innovative · game-changing · revolutionary

### Do Use Instead
fast · free · no login · works · instant · ephemeral · private · browser-native · open URL · sync

## 3. CONTENT PILLARS (Choose From These Angles)

Rotate through these pillars. Never post the same angle twice in a row.

### Pillar A: Building in Public (Works Best)
Share real progress, metrics, wins, and failures. This is your highest-performing format.

*Examples:*
- "I bought my first domain last week."
- "My traffic grew from 0 to 43 weekly clicks. Here's what worked."
- "I spent 3 hours debugging a Convex mutation. Here's what I learned."

### Pillar B: Technical Deep-Dive
Explain how something works under the hood. Developers love this.

*Examples:*
- "How I built Burn After Reading with Convex real-time engine."
- "Why I chose Convex over Firebase for real-time sync."
- "How WebSocket subscriptions power sub-100ms clipboard sync."

### Pillar C: Pain Point / Problem-Solution
Start with a relatable problem, then introduce the solution (not always SyncClip — can be general).

*Examples:*
- "The 'email yourself a link' workflow is broken. Here's why."
- "WhatsApp Saved Messages is not a productivity tool."
- "Why do simple utilities still require account sign-up?"

### Pillar D: Lessons & Reflections
Share what building taught you. These consistently get 800-1400 impressions.

*Examples:*
- "5 things I learned from shipping my first real product."
- "What buying a domain taught me about committing to a project."
- "Privacy isn't a feature. It's architecture."

### Pillar E: Privacy / Anti-SaaS
Tap into the growing frustration with data-hungry apps.

*Examples:*
- "I built a tool that self-destructs your data after reading."
- "Your clipboard doesn't need to be sent to a server forever."
- "Why I refuse to add login to my free tool."

### Pillar F: Industry Hot Take
Comment on current trends in a way that relates to your product.

*Examples:*
- "AI tools are collecting everything. Maybe some things should stay ephemeral."
- "The 'sign in with Google' fatigue is real."
- "Every utility app doesn't need to be a SaaS."

## 9. POSTING RULES

1. **Link goes AFTER the CTA question**, never in the body text. LinkedIn penalizes posts with external links in the body.
2. **Bold the hook** (first 1-2 lines) — use Unicode bold text converter.
3. **Never use emoji bullets** (like "👉") in story posts. Use plain paragraphs for narrative flow.
4. **Use emoji bullets** (`🔹`, `→`, `▸`) only in list/lessons posts.
5. **Keep it under 1,200 characters** for maximum "see more" rate. LinkedIn cuts off at ~150 characters in feed.
6. **Always attach a visual asset** — screenshot, mockup, or OG image. Posts with images get 2x engagement.
7. **Tag relevant people/companies** sparingly — tag Convex if the post is technical, never in marketing posts.
8. **Reply to every comment** — don't just "thanks!" them. Add value in replies. Ask follow-up questions.

## 13. CONTENT REPURPOSING WORKFLOW

One blog post → multiple LinkedIn posts:

1. **Write blog post** (1000+ words, AEO-optimized)
2. **Extract 1 key insight** → LinkedIn post (story/lessons format)
3. **Extract 1 technical detail** → LinkedIn post (technical deep-dive)
4. **Extract 1 metric or result** → LinkedIn post (mini case study)

This way, a single piece of content feeds 3 weeks of LinkedIn posts.
