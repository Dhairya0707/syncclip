What the problem actually is
Problem 1: Your title doesn't say "online clipboard"
You rank for "online clipboard" at position 9.5. But your title probably doesn't have those exact words, or emphasizes something else. When Bing shows your result for this query, the user sees your title and thinks "this isn't the direct answer" — so they click the result at position 1 instead.

Fix: Your main landing page title should START with "Online Clipboard" or "Free Online Clipboard Sync". That's the exact query.
Problem 2: Meta description doesn't answer the question
Users search "online clipboard" — they want to know what it is, fast. Your description probably talks about features (realtime sync, no login, etc). But it should lead with OUTCOME: "Sync text between devices instantly. No app. No login. Paste anywhere."

Fix: Rewrite meta description as an answer, not a feature list. Lead with the benefit.
Problem 3: You're ranking well but on the "wrong" page
These 1,200 impressions might be coming from your blog posts, /about page, or /clipboard-sync-guide, NOT your homepage. The user wants the tool itself, but you're showing them an explanation page. They bounce to a product site instead.

Check: In Bing Webmaster Tools, filter impressions by page. Which page is getting the "online clipboard" impressions? If it's not your main product page, that's the problem.
