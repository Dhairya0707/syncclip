## What is SyncClip?

**SyncClip is an online clipboard that lets you move text between devices
without installing an app or sending the text to yourself through email or
messaging apps.**

Create a clipboard session, connect another device, and paste or type your
text. Your clipboard stays available across the connected devices.

SyncClip is designed for quick text transfer between:

- 📱 Phone and PC
- 📱 Phone and Mac
- 💻 PC and Mac
- 💻 Multiple computers
- 📱 Multiple mobile devices

## How SyncClip works

### 01 — Create

Open SyncClip and create a clipboard session instantly.

**No signup. No email. Nothing to install.**

### 02 — Share

Connect your other device using the session link or scan the QR code.

One session can connect your devices.

### 03 — Paste

Type or paste text on one connected device and access it from the others.

The clipboard synchronizes across the connected devices in milliseconds.

## Why use an online clipboard?

Moving a small piece of text between devices should be simple.

You might need to:

- Copy a URL from your phone to your computer
- Move a code snippet from your PC to your phone
- Transfer notes between devices
- Share a short piece of text with another device
- Move copied text without opening a messaging app
- Transfer text when you don't want to install another application

SyncClip provides a lightweight browser-based way to handle these situations.

---

## Features

- **Cross-device clipboard** — move text between connected devices.
- **Browser-based** — use SyncClip directly from your browser.
- **No installation required** — nothing to download.
- **No signup required** — create a session and start using it.
- **QR pairing** — quickly connect another device by scanning a QR code.
- **Shareable session link** — connect devices through a shared URL.
- **Fast synchronization** — text appears across connected devices in
  milliseconds.
- **Automatic 24-hour purge** — clipboard sessions are automatically removed
  after 24 hours.
