## 📉 Legacy Alternatives (And why they are struggling)

If you are looking for other options, here is why traditional tools are falling out of favor for simple text sharing:

1.  **Pushbullet**: Once the gold standard, but now heavily gated behind paywalls and requires mandatory account creation just to sync a link.
2.  **KDE Connect**: Fantastic for Linux power users, but requires a heavy native app installation on all devices and can be finicky over certain local Wi-Fi networks.
3.  **The "Self-Email"**: The most common workaround. It clutters your inbox, creates unnecessary digital baggage, and takes 10x longer than using a dedicated online clipboard.

## 🧠 The Philosophy of Transience

Not all data needs to be archived. A temporary password or a meme link has a lifespan of 30 seconds. Treating this transient data with the heavy infrastructure of an email server or a cloud drive is massive overkill. 

By utilizing an **Online Clipboard** like SyncClip, you achieve Inbox Zero and maintain a pristine digital workspace.
