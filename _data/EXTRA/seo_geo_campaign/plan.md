## Strategy Overview (60% SEO / 40% GEO)
To rank for "online clipboard" and "clipboard share" (moving from Page 2 to Page 1), we must build **Topical Authority**. To maintain our lead in AI Citations (Bing/Perplexity), we must use a **Hub & Spoke** model where external platforms link back to our main domain.

*Product Angle to Push*: 
- Speed & Frictionless: No app install, no account required.
- Privacy: "Burn Mode" (instant privacy and ephemeral data).
