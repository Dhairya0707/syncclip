# SyncClip: Brand Voice & Persona Strategy

## 1. Strategic Direction: The "Hybrid" Approach
SyncClip does not use a single monotone voice. We use a **Hybrid Strategy** that splits the identity between a polished product and an authentic founder.

*   **The Product (SyncClip):** An authoritative, editorial, and reliable utility.
*   **The Founder (Dhairya):** An indie maker building in public, sharing honest struggles and wins.

---

## 2. Voice by Channel

| Channel | Focus | Tone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Landing Page / SEO** | Product-first | Authoritative, benefit-driven, concise. |
| **Reddit / Hacker News** | Founder-led | Humble, technical, problem-solving ("I built this because..."). |
| **LinkedIn** | Hybrid | Professional milestones + "Building in public" updates. |
| **Product Hunt** | Founder-led | Story-driven, passionate, community-focused. |
| **Blog / SEO Guides** | Editorial | Informative, structured, like a tech journal (WIRED-style). |
| **Support / Errors** | Product-first | Direct, helpful, zero fluff. |

---

## 3. The Core Voice Pillars

### 1. Confident, not Arrogant
State facts, don't use hyperbole. Let the performance speak.
*   ❌ "The world's best clipboard sync."
*   ✅ "Sub-100ms sync. No accounts required."

### 2. Precise, not Jargon-heavy
Explain the "Magic" simply. Avoid corporate "buzzwords."
*   ❌ "Leverage our robust WebSocket ecosystem for seamless data propagation."
*   ✅ "Type on your phone, see it on your laptop. Instantly."

### 3. Editorial Authority
Match the "Digital Broadsheet" design. Use a voice that sounds like it belongs in a high-end tech publication.
*   **Rule:** No exclamation marks unless absolutely necessary. No emojis on the main product UI.

### 4. Anti-SaaS Vocabulary
**Banned Words:** *Seamless, Robust, Best-in-class, Synergize, Solution, Empower, Paradigm.*

---

## 4. The Founder Persona (Dhairya)

When posting as the creator, use these three rules:
1.  **Start with the Pain:** Always mention the "Emailing myself" or "App fatigue" frustration first.
2.  **Be Transparent:** Share tech stack choices (Next.js, Convex) and why they matter for the user.
3.  **Invite, don't Sell:** Ask for "roasts," feedback, or feature requests rather than "Buy my product."

---

## 5. Copywriting Checklist
*   [ ] Is it under 20 words? (Keep it punchy)
*   [ ] Is there a "fluff" adjective I can remove?
*   [ ] Does it sound like a senior engineer wrote it?
*   [ ] If it's for a social post: Does it sound like a human wrote it?
