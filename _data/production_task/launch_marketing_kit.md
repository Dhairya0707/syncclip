## 🐱 Product Hunt (Launch Day)
**Tagline:** No-login, zero-install real-time clipboard sync between any device.
**Description:** 
SyncClip is a browser-native utility that bridges the gap between your phone, tablet, and desktop. Built on Next.js and Convex, it offers a "scratchpad" protocol that requires zero accounts and zero downloads. Just open a URL, share the 6-character code (or scan QR), and sync text/code snippets instantly.

**Maker's Comment (The Story):**
"Hi PH! I built SyncClip because I was tired of 'App Fatigue.' I didn't want to install a 50MB app or log in with my Google account just to move a URL from my phone to my laptop. 

I wanted something that felt like a digital broadsheet—clean, brutalist, and fast. SyncClip is built for students, developers, and anyone who values privacy. Your data exists only in a transient buffer and self-destructs after 24 hours (or instantly with Burn Mode). 

Excited to hear your feedback!"

## 🤖 Reddit Strategy

### r/webdev (The Tech Angle)
**Title:** I built a real-time clipboard sync with Next.js and Convex to avoid "App Fatigue"
**Body:**
Hey everyone, I recently finished SyncClip. It's a web-based clipboard bridge. I was frustrated with tools that required mobile apps or forced logins for simple text transfers.

Tech Stack:
- Frontend: Next.js (App Router)
- Backend: Convex (for that sweet WebSocket performance)
- Styling: Vanilla CSS (Brutalist aesthetic)

Happy to answer any questions about the architecture or the "Burn After Reading" implementation!

### r/selfhosted (The Privacy Angle)
**Title:** A privacy-first, ephemeral clipboard sync that doesn't track you
**Body:**
SyncClip is a no-login alternative to Pushbullet. It doesn't ask for your email or notifications. Data is stored in a transient buffer and is purged every 24 hours. If you enable "Burn Mode," the node wipes itself 5 seconds after the first remote access.
