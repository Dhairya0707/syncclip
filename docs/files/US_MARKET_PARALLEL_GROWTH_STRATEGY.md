## RISK MITIGATION: Prevent Cannibalization

### Red Flag #1: US Article Ranks Better Than /Clipboard

**Symptom:**
- Article "How to Copy Text from Phone to Computer" ranks position 3 for "copy text from phone to computer"
- But /clipboard doesn't rank for that keyword (buried at position 25+)
- Users find the article first, not the tool

**Response:**
- This is GOOD (not bad) if article links to /clipboard strongly
- Users read article → click /clipboard link → use tool
- The article acts as a feeder page
- No action needed

### Red Flag #2: US Articles Cannibalizing "Online Clipboard" Ranking

**Symptom:**
- You publish US articles in Week 7
- In Week 8, /clipboard rank drops from 5.2 to 7.5
- "Online clipboard" CTR drops from 0.24% to 0.18%

**Response (Immediate):**
1. Check: Did Google confuse your articles with /clipboard?
   - In GSC, see which pages rank for "online clipboard"
   - If articles appear, reduce their internal linking to /clipboard
2. Wait 7 days (Google recalculates)
3. If rank doesn't recover: Reduce new article publishing temporarily

**Prevention:**
- Keep US article anchors specific: "sync phone to computer" (not "online clipboard")
- Don't over-link: 3–4 links per article max
- Vary anchor text (don't use "online clipboard" in every link)

### Red Flag #3: US Articles Get 0 Clicks After 30 Days

**Symptom:**
- Article published, indexed, ranking at position 18
- After 30 days: still position 18–20, zero clicks

**Response:**
- Check: Is the article's target keyword actually searched?
  - Example: "copy text from phone to computer" = 668 impressions, your article should get some
  - If not getting impressions: Article not ranking for the query (optimization issue)
- Fix: Improve article SEO
  - Add keyword to H1, first paragraph, meta title
  - Improve internal links (more links to /clipboard)
  - Consider building backlinks (external sites linking to article)
- Timeline: Give it 60 days total before deciding to consolidate
