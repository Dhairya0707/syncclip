## Core Product Vision

SyncClip is a premium, real-time synchronization tool built to eliminate the friction of moving text, code, and links between your devices. It replaces clunky workflows (like emailing yourself snippets) with a **Digital Bridge** that is always ready, completely private, and works in real-time.

* **No-Login Experience**: Secure 6-character room codes act as the room identifiers.
* **Ephemeral Storage**: Sessions auto-expire and clear from Convex after 24 hours.
* **Burn-After-Reading**: Arm a session to self-destruct once read by a second device.
* **Keyboard-First Design**: Integrated Command Palette (`CMD + K`) for power users.
