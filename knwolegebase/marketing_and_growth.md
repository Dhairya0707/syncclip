# Marketing & Growth Playbook

This document details the user acquisition plan, social media copywriting system, free directory listings, and visual brand guidelines used to grow SyncClip without paid ad budgets.

---

## 1. Core Marketing Narrative

SyncClip positions itself not as a SaaS dashboard, but as a **lightweight, utility-first digital bridge**. Marketing content should always focus on solving the direct friction of self-messaging.

### The Value Pillars
1. **"Copy Here. Paste Anywhere."** (The single-sentence hook).
2. **"No Signups, No Accounts, No Trackers."** (Friction elimination).
3. **"Burn After Reading Security."** (Appealing to privacy-conscious developers).

---

## 2. Brand Asset Directory & Visual Design Rules

To ensure social graphics are consistent, use the following assets and design settings:

### Primary Creative Elements
* **The Monogram**: SC interlocking monogram in Action Blue (`#057DBC`) on a white background. Used for profile pictures and social icons.
* **The 3-Step Flow Diagrams**:
  1. `step-1-create.png` (Create: Instant room generator readout).
  2. `step-2-join.png` (Share: QR scan or code insertion window).
  3. `step-3-sync.png` (Paste: Live text propagation).
* **Social Image / OG Banner**: `og-image.png` (1200×630). Standard high-contrast broadsheet layout with serif titles.

### Graphic Composition Rules
* **Borders**: 1px to 2px solid black borders, `0px` border-radius.
* **Fonts**: `Newsreader` (Serif for titles), `Space Grotesk` (Sans-serif for captions), `JetBrains Mono` (Monospace for tech kickers).
* **Colors**: Pure white canvas (`#ffffff`), pure black text (`#000000`), select highlighter marks in Action Blue (`#057dbc`).

## 3. The LinkedIn "Builder Angle" Playbook

LinkedIn outreach works best when formatted as a personal builder journey rather than a corporate press release. 

### Launch Copy Framework
* **The Hook**: A visceral, specific opening statement (e.g., `I bought my first domain last week.`).
* **The Story**: Acknowledge the developer's personal pain points (e.g., emailing links to yourself or using WhatsApp "Saved Messages" as an ad-hoc clipboard).
* **The Solution**: Explain the technology simply (e.g., Next.js + Convex websocket subscriptions, syncing in under 100ms).
* **Call to Action (CTA)**: An open-ended question to spark conversation (e.g., `What was the first thing you shipped to production?`).
* **Formatting Rules**: Avoid emoji bullets. Use Unicode bold converters (`𝗜 𝗯𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵𝘁 𝗺𝘆 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗱𝗼𝗺𝗮𝗶𝗻...`) only for the primary hook and first mention of **SyncClip**. Keep paragraphs short and space-separated.

## 4. Community Outreach & Directory Submissions

Launch campaigns are distributed across developers and early adopters:

### Reddit Strategy
* **Target Communities**: `r/webdev`, `r/sideprojects`, `r/devtools`, `r/selfhosted`.
* **Post Format**: Image/video posts displaying the desktop-and-mobile side-by-side QR pairing and instant text sync.
* **Narrative Angle**: Post as a student builder asking for feedback on the "Editorial Brutalist" UX. Avoid sales language.

### Hacker News (Show HN)
* **Title**: `Show HN: SyncClip – An online clipboard with zero login or install`
* **Intro Comment**: Focus on technical choices (Next.js, Convex real-time subscriptions) and the privacy implementation (burn mode, 24h volatile lifecycle).

### Free Software Directories
Submit SyncClip as a privacy-friendly, zero-login alternative to tools like Pushbullet or pastebins:
* **uneed.app**
* **Peerlist**
* **Fazier**
* **AlternativeTo** (List under Pushbullet alternatives)
* **Product Hunt** (Under the "Developer Tools" category)

### Developer Build Logs (Dev.to / Medium)
Write articles detailing the developer journey:
* *Title Idea*: "How I Built a Real-Time Clipboard Sync Tool in 2 Weeks with Convex and Next.js"
* *Focus*: Explain the cursor-jumping fix, Convex schemas, and real-time database subscription benefits.
